D1 Sat, Nov 07 • Arrival & Check-in

19:50 - 22:50 Flight to Bangkok

Depart on Thai Vietjet Air (VZ569) to Suvarnabhumi Airport (BKK).

23:00 - 00:00 Customs & Baggage

Clear immigration and claim checked baggage (20kg).

00:00 - 00:45 Transit to City

Take a Grab or Taxi heading to the city center.

~ 01:00 Check-in at Aira Hotel

Located in the lively Nana area. Grab a quick late-night snack nearby, settle into your room, and rest up for tomorrow's big day!

D2 Sun, Nov 08 • The Birthday Celebration 🎂

09:30 - 10:15 BTS Eastbound

Take the BTS from Nana directly to Bang Na station.

10:30 - 12:00 Target Practice

Live-fire shooting experience at Ratchanawi Bang Na Shooting Range. Kick off the birthday with a bang!

12:30 - 14:00 Premium Wagyu Lunch

Take the BTS back to Ekkamai and treat the birthday VIP to top-tier wagyu beef bowls at CHUNN.

14:15 - 17:00 Rest & Dress Up

Head back to Aira Hotel (Nana). Shower, recharge, and change into your smart casual birthday outfit.

17:30 - 20:30 Sunset Birthday Dinner

Take the BTS to Ploenchit. Enjoy refined Thai cuisine with cultural performances and stunning sunset city views at Akara Sky Hanuman.

21:00 - 23:30 Rooftop Drinks

BTS to Thong Lo. Toast to the new year under the iconic color-changing jellyfish tree at Tichuca Rooftop Bar.

23:45 - 02:00 F1 Midnight Party

BTS to Phrom Phong. Arrive at Raze Arcade (Emsphere 5F) just in time for the midnight lights out of the Brazilian GP!

D3 Mon, Nov 09 • Culture & City Lights

10:30 - 11:30 Morning Coffee

Sleep in, then take the BTS to Phaya Thai for champion-level brews at Factory Coffee - BKK.

11:45 - 13:15 LV The Place Bangkok

BTS to Chit Lom. Explore the immersive Louis Vuitton exhibition at Gaysorn Amarin.

13:30 - 14:00 Transit to Old Town

Grab a taxi to the riverside. Don't forget to pick up K. Panich Sticky Rice along the way to curb your appetite!

14:00 - 16:00 Wat Arun Thai Dress

Rent traditional garments and get your makeup done in the alleyways, then snap premium photos at the stunning white pagoda (Temple of Dawn).

16:15 - 17:30 ICONSIAM & Snacks

Take the cross-river ferry. Explore the indoor floating market (SookSiam), and grab After You dessert or ChaTraMue Thai tea.

18:00 - 18:45 Transit to Banthat Thong

Taxi to the famous food street (Chulalongkorn area).

19:00 - 21:00 Dinner: The Mama Noodle Tactic

Grab a queue ticket for Jeh O Chula, eat Nueng Nom Nua toast while waiting, or pivot to Here Duan curry rice if the line is too long!

D4 Tue, Nov 10 • Departure

05:30 - 06:15 Check-out & Airport Transit

Early check-out and taxi to BKK Airport. (Tip: Bangkok morning traffic is heavy, depart by 05:30!).

06:15 - 08:30 Check-in & Security

Baggage drop and customs clearance.

09:05 - 13:45 Flight to Taipei

Depart on Thai Vietjet Air (VZ568) and head home safely with great memories.

Cultural Heritage

The Grand Palace

The official residence of the Kings of Siam. Breathtaking architecture.

Iconic Landmark
Wat Pho

Famous for the massive Reclining Buddha.

Wat Arun

The Temple of Dawn. Known for its stunning riverside location and intricate details. Perfect for Thai dress photos.

Modern City & Shopping

ICONSIAM

Massive luxury riverside mall featuring an impressive indoor floating market (SookSiam).

EmQuartier

Luxury shopping complex in Sukhumvit with a stunning indoor waterfall.

King Power Mahanakhon

Thailand's highest observation deck offering 360-degree panoramic city views and a glass floor.

City View
LV The Place Bangkok

Immersive Louis Vuitton destination at Gaysorn Amarin.

Activities & Leisure

Raze Arcade

High-end entertainment hub with state-of-the-art racing simulators. F1 viewing venue.

Ratchanawi Bang Na Shooting Range

Professional indoor shooting range offering real firearm experiences.

Rooftop Bars & Premium Views

Tichuca Rooftop Bar

Iconic jungle-themed rooftop famous for its massive, color-changing LED jellyfish tree installation.

Vibe & View
Le Du Kaan

Elevated modern Thai dining by a Michelin-starred chef, located at the top of Empire Tower.

Dining with a View
Akara Sky Hanuman

A unique rooftop bar blending spectacular city views with traditional Thai cultural performances.

TRIBE Sky Beach Club

A tropical oasis above the city featuring a pool, vibrant atmosphere, and great cocktails.

TYME Rooftop

A more relaxed, stylish rooftop space perfect for evening drinks and conversation.

Michelin & Local Legends

Jeh O Chula

Legendary late-night spot famous for their loaded Tom Yum Mama noodles.

Bib Gourmand
K. Panich Sticky Rice

Serving traditional, perfectly crafted mango sticky rice for over 90 years in the Old Town.

Bib Gourmand
Thipsamai Padthai

Often hailed as the best Pad Thai in Bangkok, wrapped in a thin egg omelet.

Bib Gourmand
Phed Mark

Co-founded by famous food YouTuber Mark Wiens. Specializes in intense, authentic Pad Krapow (Holy Basil Stir-fry).

Must Try

Mae Varee

No-frills, authentic local spot serving fresh and spicy Thai seafood dishes.

CHUNN

Cozy eatery specializing in premium beef rice bowls and perfectly grilled meats.
